About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the AIR (Artificial Intelligence and Rendering) research group, at the Department of Information Technologies and Systems of the University of Castilla-La Mancha (UCLM), and I work at the School of Computer Science in Ciudad Real, Spain. I study how artificial intelligence can help physiotherapists personalise the rehabilitation of patients with neurological conditions, always leaving the final decision to them.

In my PhD I designed a fuzzy-logic decision support system that adjusts each patient's routines according to how they perform them and explains each recommendation to the physiotherapist, and a model that estimates the patient's fatigue during virtual reality exercises and compares it with the patient's self-perception. Both come together in Physio Galenus, an open-source platform. I designed them with therapists at the Hospital Nacional de Parapléjicos in Toledo and we tested them with their patients.

During my research stay at Aston University (United Kingdom) I coupled a language model to this system, with a calibrated estimate of the uncertainty of each proposal. I also work with locally deployed language models to generate patient-oriented clinical sheets from teleconsultations.

I did my PhD under an industrial doctorate contract from the UCLM Plan Propio, co-funded by the ESF+: the first year at Furious Koalas, the UCLM spin-off where I had already worked as a full-stack engineer, and the following years at the university. I defended my thesis on 15 October 2026.

Software

I release the code of my research as open-source software (MIT licence), with a DOI on Zenodo.

  • Physio Galenus

    Web platform where physiotherapists manage patients and game-based exercise routines. A fuzzy-logic decision support system proposes adjustments and explains each recommendation.

    GitHubZenodoArticle
  • Fatigue-Detector-Rehab

    Adaptive model that estimates the patient's fatigue during virtual reality rehabilitation exercises.

    GitHubZenodoArticle
  • RehabImmersive-Kinematic-Analysis

    Browser-based tool for the post hoc kinematic analysis of upper-limb exercises performed in immersive virtual reality.

    GitHubZenodoArticle
  • ReActivate

    Augmented reality architecture for developing and deploying exergames that promote and guide physical activity in older adults.

    GitHubZenodoPreprint
